## When to use it

You want a quick, visible change to some text, to test a workflow or just for fun. A word that starts with a vowel gets "way" on the end. Any other word moves its opening consonants to the end and adds "ay", so "hello" becomes "ellohay". Punctuation at the ends of a word stays put, and words with no letters, like numbers, are left alone.
